---
name: Referral commission policy
description: User-confirmed trigger and settlement rules for three-tier referral commissions.
---

Default Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 rates are 20%, 3%, and 1%, respectively, once per verified investment purchase funded by verified wallet balance. Authorized Admins may change future rates; historical commissions retain their original snapshots. Settle paid commissions through funded wallet credits, not direct GCash commission payouts.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected verified investment purchases and funded wallet credits. They require eligible verified transactions only and prohibit fake earnings or unfunded spendable balances.

**How to apply:** Preserve original referral relationships and avoid duplicate commission entries. Pending, rejected, cancelled, or duplicate transactions must not generate commissions. Do not treat pending or approved commission amounts as spendable wallet funds; a paid credit must have verified funding.

Fund the commission reserve from verified retained withdrawal fees and separately verified company capital, never member deposit principal or invested funds. The reserve starts at zero; insufficient funds leave commissions Pending.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected both verified funding sources and authorized future rate changes. Member principal is not company commission funding.

**How to apply:** Fees qualify only after the actual payout is completed and verified. Separate company funding requires evidence and independent Admin confirmation; prevent the same bank transfer/receipt from also crediting a member deposit. Retain capital evidence during upload cleanup.
