name: Referral commission policy
description: User-confirmed trigger and settlement rules for three-tier referral commissions.
---

Default Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 rates are 25%, 3%, and 1%, respectively, once per successfully verified deposit after the policy switch. New investment purchases must not also generate commissions. Historical investment commissions retain their original source and rate snapshots, including the previous 20% Tier 1 rate. Authorized Admins may change future rates. Settle paid commissions through funded wallet credits, not direct GCash commission payouts.

**Why:** The user replaced the investment-purchase policy with verified-deposit commissions at 25% / 3% / 1% and explicitly chose future verifications only, without backfilling past approved deposits. The funded-wallet settlement and verified company funding choices remain unchanged.

**How to apply:** Preserve original referral relationships and avoid duplicate commission entries per deposit and tier/recipient. Use the actual verified amount, not the member's requested amount; require both approval audit and the active deposit's completed ledger credit. Pending, rejected, cancelled, or duplicate transactions must not generate commissions. Do not treat pending or approved commission amounts as spendable wallet funds; a paid credit must have verified funding. Qualified totals count distinct invited members with a verified deposit; earned totals count completed funded wallet credits only.

Fund the commission reserve from verified retained withdrawal fees and separately verified company capital, never member deposit principal or invested funds. The reserve starts at zero; insufficient funds leave commissions Pending.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected both verified funding sources and authorized future rate changes. Member principal is not company commission funding.

**How to apply:** Fees qualify only after the actual payout is completed and verified. Separate company funding requires evidence and independent Admin confirmation; prevent the same bank transfer/receipt from also crediting a member deposit. Retain capital evidence during upload cleanup.
